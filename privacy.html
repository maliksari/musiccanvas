MusicCanvas – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-07

MusicCanvas has no accounts and no advertising, and it never sells or shares your data for tracking. Your drawings and music stay on your device. The only data that leaves the app is described under “Purchases and usage” below. The app does not connect to any server of ours.

Purchases and usage

Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Apple and by RevenueCat, our purchase-management provider (RevenueCat privacy policy). RevenueCat receives your purchase history and an anonymous, randomly generated app user ID; it does not receive your name, email or Apple ID.

Together with that anonymous ID, the app sends a few usage milestones to RevenueCat: when you finish the intro, first draw, first hear music, view or close the upgrade screen, use up the free preview, or buy or restore Pro, with a counter and the date. We use this only to understand and improve the app. It contains none of your drawings, music, photos or audio, and it is not used for advertising or tracking.

Your drawings and music

Drawings, songs and settings are stored only on your device and are removed when you delete the app. Exports (video, audio, MIDI, ringtones) are created on your device and only leave it when you choose to share or save them.

Microphone

If you use “Hum → Draw”, the microphone is used to detect the pitch of your humming in real time on your device. No audio is recorded or stored.

Photos

Photos you pick for “Music from Photo” or as a canvas background are processed on your device. Saving a video to Photos uses add-only access.

SharePlay

If you start “Draw Together”, the strokes and genre of the shared canvas are sent through Apple’s SharePlay only to the people in your FaceTime call. Nothing is sent to us.

Notifications

Daily theme reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device.

Children

MusicCanvas has no accounts, ads or tracking, and collects no personal details such as names or contact information.
