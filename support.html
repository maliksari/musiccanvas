Quick answers

How does drawing become music? Height sets the pitch, time runs from left to right, colors set the key and mood, and each brush is an instrument (pen = melody, watercolor = harmony, crayon = bass, rhythm brush = drums).

I hear a delay with Bluetooth headphones. Bluetooth adds latency; use the speaker or wired headphones for live drawing.

Hum → Draw doesn’t hear me. Allow microphone access in Settings → MusicCanvas, and hum a little louder and steadily.

Where are my exports? Use the share sheet in Export, or “Save Video to Photos”.
